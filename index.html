Sentence Movers · AR

Passive Voice

Swap the spotlight from the doer to the receiver. Five sentences, rebuilt one word at a time.

How to play
  • Prop your phone up so your face is in frame — the question floats above your head.
  • Read the active sentence, then tap the words in the tray at the bottom of the screen, in order, to build the passive version.
  • Tapped a word by mistake? Tap it again straight away (while it's the last word placed) to undo it.
Colour key

The teacher marked the assignments.

The assignments were marked by the teacher.

Purple — the doer. Keeps its colour when it drops to the end as “by the teacher”. The dashed box shows it's optional.

Green — the receiver. Moves from the end to the front.

Blue — the main verb. Keeps its colour but changes form (broke → broken): the same verb in a new form.

Yellow “-ed” — past tense in the active sentence. It turns pink “-ed” when the verb becomes a past participle.

Orange — the be-verb. A new piece with no match in the active sentence. It carries the tense.
